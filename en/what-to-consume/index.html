What can I consume?

The golden rule of fasting: If it doesn't spike your insulin and contains zero calories, it is allowed.

local_drink

1. Liquids (Hydration)

Water Min. 84–135 fl oz

Essential for detox and kidney function.

Black Coffee 2–4 cups

Must be black! No milk or sugar. Boosts fat burning.

Green / Black Tea 2–5 cups

Rich in antioxidants and improves focus.

Herbal Tea Unlimited

Avoid fruit teas due to potential hidden sugars.

Water calculation: 0.5 fl oz × body weight in lbs. (Add +17–34 fl oz extra during a fast).

electric_bolt

2. Electrolytes (Minerals)

To avoid "Keto Flu" (headaches, dizziness), you must replenish minerals flushed out by the kidneys.

•
Sodium (Salt)

3,000–5,000 mg/day – Mix high-quality salt into your water.

•
Potassium

1,000–3,000 mg/day – Use "Lite Salt" or potassium supplements.

•
Magnesium

300–450 mg/day – Best: Citrate or Glycinate.

Calculation: 2.7 mg × body weight in lbs.

medication

3. Vitamins

•
Vitamin C: 500–1,000 mg

Helps manage cortisol and stress.

•
Vitamin B6 / B-Complex: As directed

Essential for energy, but may cause nausea on an empty stomach. Take in divided doses.

warning

WARNING: If you experience constant dizziness, muscle tremors, or heart palpitations, stop your fast immediately and replenish your electrolytes!
